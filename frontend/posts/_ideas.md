## Formats that work well

- **Weeknote**: what happened this week (low bar, high value)
- **TIL**: one thing you learned, a few paragraphs
- **Project update**: what you built/shipped and why
- **FO (finished object)**: knitting project writeup
- **Talk recap**: what you presented and the conversation it sparked
- **Link post**: something you read with your take on it
